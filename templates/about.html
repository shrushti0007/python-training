🏫 About Government Polytechnic Hingoli

Government Polytechnic Hingoli is a Government Diploma Institute established in 2009 under the Directorate of Technical Education, Government of Maharashtra. The institute is approved by AICTE and affiliated with Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE). The college is committed to providing quality technical education, modern laboratories and practical learning to students.
